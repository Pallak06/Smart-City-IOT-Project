# Scope of the Solution

The scope of this Smart City IoT simulation includes the following key modules:

---

## 1. Smart Surveillance System
- Deployment of IP-based smart cameras
- Motion detection and alert triggering
- Video monitoring simulation
- Data communication with a central IoT server

---

## 2. Intelligent Street Lighting
- Light sensors and motion sensors controlling street lamps
- Lamps automatically turning ON/OFF based on movement or time of day
- Energy-efficient behavior simulated through automation

---

## 3. Fire Detection and Monitoring
- Smoke and temperature sensors installed in residential/commercial zones
- Real-time detection of abnormal conditions
- IoT server receiving and logging fire alerts for emergency response

---

## 4. IoT Network Infrastructure
- Integration of IoT devices through an IoT Gateway
- Router, switch, cloud, and wireless communication setup
- Server configured to handle device registration and M2M communication

---

## 5. Smart City Visual Layout
- Proper arrangement of buildings, roads, streetlights, sensors, and cameras
- Logical placement of devices for realistic city simulation

---

## Out of Scope (Not included)
- Real video streaming
- Real emergency services integration
- 3D mapping or GPS-based navigation
